# Saporo — Value props & proof points (from public site)

All from https://www.saporo.io/product/overview unless noted. Product-metric
claims are Saporo's own; the market stats at the bottom are Saporo-cited
industry numbers whose primary sources must be found before an article cites
them (Researcher phase rule: no pyramid-citing).

## Capability pillars (verbatim names)

1. **Unified identity visibility** — complete view across identities,
   permissions, relationships, and critical assets
2. **Reveal attack opportunities** — connect identities and
   misconfigurations into exploitable attack paths
3. **Focus on what matters most** — identify critical chokepoints and
   high-impact exposures
4. **Prove real-world risk** — validate exploitability within attack paths
   and High Value Targets
5. **Remediate at scale** — automate through guided workflows and
   integrations ("1,900+ integrations across security, IT, and cloud
   platforms")
6. **Measure exposure reduction** — track attack resistance and compliance
   improvements
