## Pains the site presses on (verbatim-sourced, product/overview)

- Identity sprawl across human and non-human identities
- Lists and audits that don't show exploitability ("Compliance is a starting
  point, not a measure of exploitability" — blog post title)
- Attackers needing only one path while defenders must secure everything
- Slow detection/resolution of identity-based breaches (site cites 292 days
  average — Saporo-cited industry stat; find primary source before using in
  an article)

## Decision criteria implied by the product pillars

Unified visibility, exploitability proof (not just findings), prioritization
by impact on critical assets, remediation at scale via integrations,
measurable exposure reduction over time.
