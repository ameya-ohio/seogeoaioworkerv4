# Saporo — Positioning

## One-sentence positioning (their words)

> "Reduce identity exposure before it becomes an attack"
— homepage hero, https://www.saporo.io/

## Three-sentence positioning (assembled from their own copy)

Saporo delivers Preemptive Identity Exposure Management (PIEM) by continuously
discovering identity exposure across hybrid environments, including human and
non-human identities (homepage). The platform models identity connections from
an adversary's perspective, revealing how exposure could be exploited
(https://www.saporo.io/about/company). It connects identities, permissions, and
misconfigurations into exploitable attack paths, prioritizes them by
reachability, propagation potential, and impact to critical assets, and drives
remediation through guided workflows and integrations
(https://www.saporo.io/product/overview).

## Category

**Preemptive Identity Exposure Management (PIEM)** — Saporo's own category
label, used in the og:title ("Saporo – Preemptive Identity Exposure Management
(PIEM)"). Adjacent recognized categories: identity attack surface management,
attack path management, identity security posture management (ISPM — a term
their blog uses).

## Primary differentiation (verbatim claims, homepage + product overview)

- Unified visibility across hybrid, on-prem, cloud, and SaaS environments
  (Active Directory, Azure, AWS, GCP, M365, SaaS identities)
- Graph-powered attack path intelligence connecting identities, permissions,
  and misconfigurations
- Prioritization by reachability, propagation potential, and impact to
  critical assets
- "1,900+ integrations" for automated remediation workflows
- Contextual risk scoring validated against ANSSI, CIS, ISO, and MITRE
  frameworks
- Covers human **and non-human** identities
