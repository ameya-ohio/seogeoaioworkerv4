# Format guide: Integration Page

**The job.** Answer "does it integrate with X, and what does it need?", the technical question that blocks deals.

**Held** until a fact sheet exists at `context/product/integration-page/<slug>.md` (D51) with the exact permissions, setup path and limitations.

## Structure
1. **H1:** "[Platform] Integration".
2. **Intro (answer block).** Built from the research Topic Summary ¶1–2 (D44); paragraph 1 carries the answer block below within its first 60–80 words, paragraph 2 the sourced why-now. The answer block says what the integration does and what it requires, in 50 words.
3. What it enables: 3–5 capabilities specific to this platform.
4. What data is collected (read-only where true), and **the exact permission or role names required**.
5. Setup as numbered steps with the real console path (HowTo schema).
6. Architecture: agentless, API-based, collection frequency.
7. What you'll see, and limitations: what isn't covered, named honestly.
Then **Key Takeaways** (the count in page.md), declarative H2s throughout (D33), the company section weighted by the funnel, the **FAQ** within page.md's range, and the close with page.md's CTA.

Use the platform's own vocabulary exactly as the platform documents it.

## Fails when
Permissions are vague, setup is generic, or limitations are hidden.
