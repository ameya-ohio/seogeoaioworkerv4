# Format guide: Solution Page

**The job.** Convert a buyer who knows what they need. Short, specific, conversion-shaped, and still readable by a model.

**Held** until a fact sheet exists at `context/product/solution-page/<slug>.md` (D51): exact capabilities, proof and deployment facts come from the company, not from research.

## Structure
1. **H1:** the solution in the buyer's words.
2. **Intro (answer block).** Built from the research Topic Summary ¶1–2 (D44); paragraph 1 carries the answer block below within its first 60–80 words, paragraph 2 the sourced why-now. The answer block says what it does, for whom, with what outcome, in 50 words.
3. The problem, in the buyer's language.
4. 3–5 capability blocks, each framed as an outcome.
5. Proof: named customers where the fact sheet permits, with quantified outcomes.
6. How it works in 4–6 steps, what's included, deployment, and integrations.
Then **Key Takeaways** (the count in page.md), declarative H2s throughout (D33), the company section weighted by the funnel, the **FAQ** within page.md's range, and the close with page.md's CTA.

## Fails when
It's a feature list, the proof is unquantified, or it has no FAQ.
