# Format guide: Checklist

**The job.** An exhaustive, orderable list of verifiable items. A checklist *is* a list-shaped answer, which AI Overviews extract directly.

**Query patterns.** "X checklist", "X audit checklist", "X hardening checklist", "X RFP checklist".

## Structure
1. **H1:** "[Topic] Checklist".
2. **Intro (answer block).** Built from the research Topic Summary ¶1–2 (D44); paragraph 1 carries the answer block below within its first 60–80 words, paragraph 2 the sourced why-now. The answer block says "This checklist covers N items across M areas: …" and who it is for.
3. How to use it: scoring, and what a pass looks like.
4. **The checklist, grouped into 4–8 named sections.** Each item is a yes/no imperative with one line of why, pointing to the page that explains it.
5. Priority tiers: critical, important, recommended.
6. Scoring and what your result means, then what to do with your findings.
Then **Key Takeaways** (the count in page.md), declarative H2s throughout (D33), the company section weighted by the funnel, the **FAQ** within page.md's range, and the close with page.md's CTA.

The company section says honestly which items the product checks continuously ("24 of these 40 run automatically").

## Fails when
Items are vague ("review permissions"), unverifiable, or ungrouped. Every item must be answerable yes or no by someone at the console.
