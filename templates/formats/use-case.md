# Format guide: Use Case

**The job.** Let a specific buyer see themselves: queries qualified by role, industry or scenario that broad pages can't reach.

**Held** until a fact sheet exists at `context/product/use-case/<slug>.md` (D51).

## Structure
1. **H1:** "[Solution] for [Segment or Scenario]".
2. **Intro (answer block).** Built from the outline's Intro Strategy, which starts from the thesis (D60); paragraph 1 carries the answer block below within its first 60–80 words, and paragraph 2 says why it matters now: a change or a consequence that supports the thesis, with at most one figure and only one the argument turns on. The answer block states this segment's specific problem and the outcome.
3. Why this segment is different: constraints that are only true for it (regulation, legacy estate, staffing, uptime tolerance).
4. The segment's specific risks, and how the product applies (3–4 capabilities framed to the segment).
5. The compliance mapping that binds this segment (ANSSI, NIS2, DORA, ISO 27001, HIPAA…).
6. Proof from a customer in this segment, and deployment considerations.
Then **Key Takeaways** (the count in page.md), declarative H2s throughout (D33), the company section weighted by the funnel, the **FAQ** within page.md's range, and the close with page.md's CTA.

## Fails when
It's the solution page with the industry name swapped in.
