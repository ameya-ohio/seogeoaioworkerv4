# Research playbook: Pillar

**Formats:** Pillar Guide.

**What the page needs.** A complete guide to the whole topic that answers each sub-theme fully and ties them into one argument. Its child pages go deeper, but the pillar can't be a list of summaries. It needs a spine: the position that explains why these sub-themes belong together and in what order a reader should think about them. Your research supplies the defining mechanism of each sub-theme and the evidence for the connecting position.

## Gather this (→ `## Subject Material`)

### Sub-themes
One entry per child page or sub-theme in the brief, in the brief's order:

```
- **<Sub-theme>** (child page: <title>)
  - Core mechanism: how it works, in two or three sentences, with its primary source
  - The decision it drives: what a reader does differently once they understand it
  - Depth lives in: the child page (don't research the child's full treatment)
```

### How the sub-themes connect
The dependencies between them: which one has to be understood first, and which one changes how another should be done. Name the mechanism behind each connection.

### The whole-topic definition
The definition of the topic from standards or the originating framework, and how the term is used inconsistently in the market.

### Landscape
How the topic has changed recently: new standards, new attack classes, category shifts. Cite each change to its primary source.

## Prefer these sources
Standards and frameworks, primary technical documentation for each sub-theme's mechanism, government advisories, and the most-cited neutral references on the topic.

## Don't collect
- Each child page's full research. The pillar needs each sub-theme's core, not its detail.
- A statistic per sub-theme.

## Candidate positions for this mode
The connecting argument: why these sub-themes form one discipline, and the order that matters. A pillar without a spine reads as a table of contents.

## Statistics & Data Points for this mode
Three to five across the whole guide, each tied to the connecting argument or to the sub-theme it sizes.

## Done when
- Every sub-theme in the brief has a sourced core mechanism and the decision it drives.
- The connections between sub-themes are written.
