# Research playbook: Commercial

**Formats:** Solution Page, Offer / Landing Page.

**What the page needs.** The buyer's problem, stated in their terms, and proof that the company solves it. Most of the material is internal: `context/sales/proof-points.md` (Citable: yes only), `context/case-studies/`, `context/brand/`, and `config/company.yaml`. Web research is light and supports the problem statement.

## Gather this (→ `## Subject Material`)

### The problem
What the buyer is trying to get done, what gets in the way, and what it costs them. Use one documented case or advisory that shows the cost, if one exists.

### Proof
The proof points and case studies that apply, quoted from `context/` with their file names and publishing boundaries. Never a number from anywhere else.

### Objections
The questions a buyer asks before acting (from `context/sales/`, or public forums if context has none), each with the answer the company can support.

### Alternatives the buyer considers
The approaches or categories the buyer weighs instead, framed as categories. Name a head-to-head vendor only where the competitor rules allow it.

## Prefer these sources
The company's own context files first, then advisories or neutral analyses that document the problem.

## Don't collect
- Market-size figures and generic threat statistics.
- Competitor material of any kind.

## Candidate positions for this mode
Why the buyer's current approach falls short, in one sentence the proof points can back.

## Statistics & Data Points for this mode
Zero to two external figures. Company numbers come only from `proof-points.md`.

## Done when
- The problem and the proof are written, and each proof item names its context file.
