## Topic Summary

Three Sales Playbook AI Workers turn proposal-and-ROI work from a 2-3 day post-call task into a live, in-meeting deliverable: the Proposal Generator (call recording → branded proposal in your design template), the Business Case Generator (live ROI build with prospect on screen), the RFP Responder (vector memory of past responses). Documents become collaborative artifacts rather than asynchronous deliverables.

The wedge: most "AI proposal generation" content describes async post-meeting drafting. EverWorker's Sales Playbook does it live, with the prospect, on the call.
