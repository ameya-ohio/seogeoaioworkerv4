## Full Outline

### Intro (≈ 200 words)
- Hook: industry timeline range vs 45 days; the difference is templates.
- Thesis preview: week-by-week plan; what's in / what's not.

### Key Takeaways (5 bullets)
- A 45-day AI-first sales rollout breaks into four phases: Weeks 1–2 Foundation, Weeks 2–3 AI SDR, Weeks 3–4 Sales Playbook Workers, Weeks 4–6 Scale & Optimize.
- 45 days is real because the templates already exist; the work is configuration, not invention.
- The thing that slows most rollouts isn't AI engineering. It's foundation work: undocumented ICP, dirty CRM, missing sending infrastructure.
- Meetings start booking in week 3. ROI clock starts there, not at the end of the rollout.
- What's in 45 days: full inbound + outbound on email. What's not: LinkedIn (Phase 2, day 90+) and expanded workers (Phase 3).

### H2 1: Why most rollouts take six months (≈ 320 words)
- Direct answer: "Most published AI SDR rollout timelines say 60 to 90 days from scratch because they're estimating from teams that haven't done the foundation work."
- Walk through industry consensus: Lindy/Autobound/IBM/Monday all give ranges, all conditional on data quality.
- The hidden variable: how long the foundation work takes. For a team with documented ICP, clean CRM, and existing sending infrastructure, it's a week. For a team starting from nothing, it's a month.
- The 30–60 day fade pattern (Salesmotion) is what happens when teams rush past the foundation. Rolling out fast without foundation = a 45-day rollout that fails by day 90.
- Quotable: "The thing that takes most teams 60 to 90 days is week 1, not week 6."

### H2 2: The 45-day rollout, week by week (≈ 1,400 words across 4 H3s)

#### H3: Weeks 1–2 — Foundation (≈ 400 words)
- Direct answer: "The foundation phase locks in the inputs every downstream worker depends on. Sending infrastructure, CRM hygiene, and documentation."
- Checklist (Sending Infrastructure):
  - 54 sending domains provisioned (50/50 Google/Microsoft split)
  - SPF/DKIM/DMARC configured per provider
  - 27-hour managed turnaround via ScaledMail (vs 32+ hours of self-managed GTM engineering)
  - Single-tenant IPs assigned
  - Private warmup pool started
  - Sequencing tool (Instantly, Lemlist, or Email Bison) connected
- Checklist (CRM):
  - Pipeline stages defined and enforced
  - Required properties at each stage documented
  - Status fields set up for AI worker writes
  - Sequencing tool integration tested
  - (More in [B2 — How to set up your CRM for AI workers](/blog/how-to-set-up-crm-for-ai-workers))
- Checklist (Documentation):
  - ICP documented with firmographic, technographic, and behavioral criteria (more in [A3](/blog/how-to-document-icp-for-ai-worker))
  - Persona profiles per buyer type
  - Messaging assets per persona × segment
  - Product/solution documentation in a live system (Confluence, Notion, SharePoint, Google Drive)
  - Case studies with named companies, named outcomes, named numbers
  - Competitive battle cards
- Decision points: pick sequencer, pick CRM if not already on HubSpot/Salesforce, pick knowledge-base host.

#### H3: Weeks 2–3 — Build the AI SDR (≈ 380 words)
- Direct answer: "With the foundation in place, the AI SDR comes online in 1.5 to 2 weeks."
- Checklist:
  - Signal definitions written for the ICP (hiring, funding, leadership, technology, displacement, product launches)
  - Signal-detection workers configured against primary sources (job boards, company websites, LinkedIn, news, Crunchbase)
  - Knowledge Engine connected (live integration to Confluence/Notion/SharePoint)
  - Research and Personalization Engine instructions written (research protocol, copywriting guidelines, hallucination guardrails, output format)
  - Intent classification logic built (5 categories: High–Active Eval, Functional, Medium-High–Category Awareness, High–Pricing Interest, Low Fit)
  - 4-email sequence templates configured (75–125w / 50–100w / 50–100w / 25–75w)
  - Subject-line rules configured (<50 chars, no spam triggers, no contact name)
  - Mandatory recency rule: only research data <12 months old
  - Reply-handling routing decided (route to human OR AI reply-handling layer)
  - Test on a small batch (50 contacts) before scale
- Reference [A1: how to build an AI SDR](/blog/how-to-build-ai-sdr) for the architectural detail.
- Quotable: "Meetings start booking in week 3. The ROI clock starts there."

#### H3: Weeks 3–4 — Build the Sales Playbook Workers (≈ 320 words)
- Direct answer: "While the AI SDR is running, build the deal-execution layer for AEs."
- Checklist:
  - Deal Velocity Sequencer configured (7-11 touches over 14-15 days; deal-execution cadence per [D1](/blog/how-to-execute-deal-cadence-ai))
  - Multi-Threading Engine wired to Clay + CRM (buying-group expansion + personalized touches per [D2](/blog/how-to-multi-thread-enterprise-deals))
  - Proposal Generator connected to call-recording tool + design template
  - Business Case Worker connected to ROI calculator template
  - RFP Responder seeded with past responses as vector memory
- Run all of these in review mode initially: AEs approve every output before it ships. Trust before scale.
- Decision points: which call-recording tool, which design platform.

#### H3: Weeks 4–6 — Scale and Optimize (≈ 300 words)
- Direct answer: "The system is live. Now you iterate based on data."
- Checklist:
  - Weekly 1-hour roundtable with the sales team (what shipped, what's working, which workers/sequences/signals are converting best)
  - Monitor signal yield and adjust definitions (drop low-yield signals, add new ones)
  - Monitor sequence performance and iterate copy
  - Monitor inbox-placement rates per domain; rotate or replace burned domains
  - Monitor CRM hygiene; close any data gaps the workers expose
  - Pull metrics: meeting booking rate by lead source, reply rate by signal type, deal-progression lift in pipeline
  - Decommission anything underperforming the baseline (5–15% inbound, 2–5% outbound)
- This is the phase that turns "live" into "compounding." Skip it and you ship a system that decays.

### H2 3: What can derail the timeline (and how to prevent it) (≈ 300 words)
- Direct answer: "The four most common derailers are: undocumented ICP, dirty CRM, missing sending infrastructure, and no clear ownership of the rollout."
- One paragraph per derailer:
  1. Undocumented ICP. Fix: dedicate week 0 to documentation if needed (push the timeline to 50 days, accept it).
  2. Dirty CRM. Fix: data cleanup happens in week 1; don't try to defer it.
  3. Missing sending infrastructure. Fix: ScaledMail or equivalent managed provisioner; don't self-manage 54 domains.
  4. No clear ownership. Fix: name a single accountable RevOps lead. Without ownership, the rollout misses week 1.
- The shared pattern: every derailer is upstream of week 2. Foundation is where rollouts succeed or fail.

### H2 4: Do I need a GTM engineer? (Question-shaped; ≈ 200 words)
- Direct answer: "You need a single accountable owner for the rollout. Whether they have 'GTM Engineer' in their title is less important than whether they own the four foundation tracks (sending infrastructure, CRM, knowledge base, sequencing tool integration) and can make decisions without escalation."
- For most teams, this person is a senior RevOps Manager or a Director of Sales Operations. Some teams have a dedicated GTM Engineer; that role is becoming standard at $50K+ ACV companies.
- What you don't need: an in-house ML team or a Python developer. The work is configuration, not engineering.

### H2 5: What "live and working" looks like at day 45 (≈ 220 words)
- Direct answer: "By day 45, the AI SDR is sequencing inbound and outbound, the Sales Playbook Workers are running every active deal, and the weekly intelligence review is in place."
- Specific outputs at day 45:
  - 100% of inbound leads prosecuted within minutes of arrival
  - Inbound conversion to meeting trending toward 5–15% (depending on lead source)
  - Outbound trending toward 2–5% on signal-triggered campaigns
  - 4,000 contacts/month sequenceable on outbound at steady state (per the [Outbound Email Infrastructure](#)/(/blog/how-to-choose-email-infrastructure-ai-sdr) plan)
  - Multi-Threading Engine running on every new deal
  - Proposals generated post-call within hours
  - Weekly roundtable cadence operating
- What's not running at day 45: LinkedIn (Phase 2, day 90+), Website SDR AI Worker, AI Reply Worker, expanded enrichment and segmentation workers (Phase 3, ongoing).
