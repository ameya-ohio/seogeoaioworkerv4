## Topic Summary

Most teams build one AI SDR workflow and shoehorn both inbound and outbound through it. The two motions have completely different inputs, different research depths, different sequence structures, different conversion benchmarks, and different success metrics. Inbound starts with the prospect raising their hand: speed-to-lead matters, qualification is the first step, the message is responsive. Outbound starts cold: signal-detection identifies the moment, research has to find a reason for the email to exist, the message is proactive.

EverWorker's SDR AI Worker Solution Guide separates these into Worker 1 (Inbound Signal SDR AI Worker) and Worker 4 (Outbound Prospecting AI Worker). The wedge is publishing the workflow design difference: when to fork into two workflows vs one shared one, what differs at each step, how to map them to the same CRM with separate AI-Worker status fields.
