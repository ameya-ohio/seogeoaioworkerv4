## Topic Summary

Most B2B sales orgs prosecute about 20% of the leads marketing generates in any given quarter. The remaining 80% get a generic 2-percent-converting sequence or nothing at all. This is not a marketing failure or a sales failure. It is a capacity failure: human SDRs cannot research, qualify, and route leads at the volume marketing produces, and the 5-minute response window inbound leads expect is incompatible with the manual workflow.

The "speed to lead" problem has been documented repeatedly: leads contacted within 5 minutes convert at materially higher rates than leads contacted at 30 minutes or longer, and the gap widens by hour. The standard industry benchmark is 21x higher conversion at 5 minutes vs 30 minutes (InsideSales.com / Lead Response Management Study, widely cited). The math has not changed; what has changed is that AI SDRs make 5-minute response possible at 100% prosecution rate.

This article publishes the speed-to-lead problem with the EverWorker proprietary 100% prosecution / 5-15% inbound conversion benchmark, and the architectural pattern that produces it.
