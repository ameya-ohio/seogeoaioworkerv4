## Topic Summary

Vendor pages quote a single blended AI SDR conversion rate. That blended rate hides where the AI SDR is producing pipeline. EverWorker's SDR AI Worker Solution Guide publishes the source-by-source benchmark: demo request up to 35%, direct contact form ~25%, webinar attended ~15%, webinar registered ~10%, content download 5-10%, outbound high-intent + signal 2-7%, outbound generic 1-3%. Wedge: per-source benchmarks plus the diagnostic for when an AI SDR is below benchmark.
