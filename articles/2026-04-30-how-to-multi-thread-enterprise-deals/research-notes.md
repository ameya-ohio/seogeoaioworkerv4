## Topic Summary

Multi-threading is the practice of engaging multiple stakeholders in a target account simultaneously rather than relying on a single champion. Saleshandy's 2026 research shows multi-threading lifts response rates by 93% versus single-contact outreach in SaaS specifically. Most teams default to single-threaded by accident: the AE develops a champion, that champion goes on holiday or moves jobs, the deal dies. The Multi-Threading Engine AI Worker fixes this by pulling additional contacts from the buying group automatically and writing personalized touches to each role.
