## Topic Summary

The Deal Velocity Problem: AEs juggle 30 active deals at once. Each needs 7-11 touches over 14-15 days for statistically significant 72% deal-progression lift. 30 deals × 7 touches × 14 days = 210 touches/2 weeks per AE, untenable for humans. So touches get missed, follow-ups dropped, deals stall in stage 2 indefinitely while the AE focuses on the four they think are about to close.

The Deal Velocity Sequencer is the AI Worker that owns the cadence. It executes on every deal, not just the four the AE remembers.
